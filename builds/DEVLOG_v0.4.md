# Devlog: DEMO v0.4

What's new since v0.3. Runs in progress carry over, and so do your unlocks and settings.

### A new look

- **The whole game has one visual style now.** Flat pixel panels, one set of fonts, one set of buttons. Your side is cobalt blue, the enemy side is warm, and Protocol is its own electric cyan everywhere it appears.
- **Every operation has its own color.** Facility Sweep is red, Hive Incursion yellow, Mantle Hunt brown, Signal Purge magenta, Veil Breach violet. It marks the enemy side: their cards, the battle count, the mission card and their pages in Help.
- **Every operation has its own dice tray.** Each one is a dark floor with its own reticle.
- Battles have no background picture any more, so the cards and dice stand out. The title screen has a new space backdrop.
- Text is crisper on phones. Card names, HP numbers and the header were redrawn to land on whole pixels.

### Title menu

- The menu is now **CONTINUE / NEW GAME / FEEDBACK**. BEGIN is called NEW GAME.
- NEW GAME over a saved run asks first. KEEP RUN keeps it, START NEW ends it.

### Squad select, rebuilt

- **A mission card** at the top: the boss, the operation's name, its level, CLEARED or LOCKED, and its threats in one line. Tap the arrows for the next operation.
- **A unit bar** under your roster shows the unit you tapped: HP, a one-line summary and a **roll bar**. The bar splits 1-20 into that unit's roll ranges, colored by what each range does (attack red, shield blue, heal green, gray for everything else), with the effect's icon under it.
- Tap a unit to add or remove it. The button reads PICK 2 MORE until your squad is full.
- Your last operation and squad are picked for you next time.

### Victory and defeat

- **OPERATION CLEARED** now has its own image for every operation, with a report of your run: battles won, survivors, XP earned, relics, turns and time.
- **OPERATION FAILED** uses the same layout and tells you where the squad fell: SQUAD LOST AT BATTLE 7/10, plus the heroes you lost, in order.

### Help

- **Five tabs: SETTINGS, BASICS, ICONS, UNITS, LOG.** Help opens on SETTINGS.
- **ICONS** is one page with every icon in the game, sorted into core, offense, defense and control, each with a one-line description.
- **UNITS** shows each unit's keywords as icons, with its evolutions grouped under it. Hold any row for the full breakdown.
- **BASICS** lists what each Protocol action costs, and REPLAY TUTORIAL is still at the bottom.

### Gameplay

- **Traits moved to 250 XP.** At 100 XP you pick an evolution as before, and it shows the two traits it can earn ("AT 250 XP: SMOLDERING or SEARING"). At 250 XP you pick one of the two. The trait becomes the hero's title on its card. Traits replace the old 250 XP Directives. Starting Directives at deploy are unchanged.
- **Enemies have no traits on your first run.** Elites and Mantle Hunt beasts gain their traits once your first run ends, win or lose.
- **Bosses always show up.** A lineup change from an intercept or a route choice can no longer replace the battle 10 boss.
- Signal Hierophant is now **Signal Hierarch** (HIERARCH on its card). Shield Enforcer's card reads SHIELD.
- Every portrait is framed the same way on every screen.
