# Devlog: DEMO v0.3

What's new since v0.2.

### Saves

- **Runs in progress are reset by this update.** The save format changed, so a run started on v0.2 cannot be continued. Your unlocks and settings are kept.
- **CONTINUE now resumes in the middle of a battle.** You come back at the round you left, with the same HP, effects and Protocol. In v0.2 it restarted the battle.
- If the browser loses the display, for example after switching apps on a phone, the game reloads and puts you back in your battle by itself.

### New: traits

- Every hero evolution and every elite enemy now has one always-on trait. It reads as a title above the unit's name on its card: SMOLDERING PYRO, BARBED STALKER, VOLATILE VOLT.
- Long-press a unit to read its trait. The evolution picker shows each choice's trait before you pick.
- A few examples. The Anchored Bulwark takes 2 less damage while taunting. The Volatile Volt deals 4 damage to every hero when it dies. The Illusory Image ignores the first hit against it.
- In Mantle Hunt every beast except the boss is Feral: when one of its allies dies, it gains rampage.

### Combat

- **Cloak is now an ambush.** A cloaked unit's next attack deals 50% more damage and ends the cloak. If every target is cloaked, a single-target attack hits one of them at random.
- **Rampage is simpler.** It doubles the unit's attack on its next turn, then ends. It no longer stacks.
- **Packs hit harder together.** Pack hunters deal 3 more damage for each other pack member still alive (was 1).
- The Geode Panther now attacks the hero whose die it freezes.
- Every unit's abilities sit on new roll ranges, and each unit's best-known move is on a wide range so you see it more often. Enemy damage and a few heroes were retuned to match.
- You never face the same enemy lineup two battles in a row.
- A reroll is now a hop in place. The die jumps and lands in its own slot instead of being thrown across the tray.

### Clearer battles

- **HP bars preview the whole round.** Your attacks, the enemies' replies, traits, rampage, pack bonuses and burn are all counted, so a bar ends where the round will leave that unit.
- A Firewall shows BLOCKED and the log says what it stopped.
- Accrete shows a chip when it adds shield, and a long-press explains it.
- A unit that does not attack now shakes in place, so every action can be seen.

### Settings and controls

- **Auto-select sole valid target:** when an action has only one valid target, it is picked for you.
- **No animations:** turns off animation that is not needed to follow the game. Dice still roll.
- Every scrolling list can be dragged with a finger.
