### Battle layout failure

`scripts/debug/battle_layout_test.gd` asserted "existing die-press cancellation preserved". The
landscape Stage A/B work had pinned the old bug on purpose (a layout task was told not to repair
it): a press on a die's hit-area was never accepted, fell through to `_unhandled_input` and
cancelled the armed Nudge. Item 7 fixed that bug (`ProtocolActions.claim_die_taps`), so the
assertion now checks the opposite: a die tap during the Nudge pick nudges that die (`6e3fce4`).

   tap. `touch_scroll_test.gd` sets `root.size = 540x1200` to avoid it. Other headless gates were
   **not** audited for the same blind spot; any gate that relies on drag tolerance, real pointer
   hit-testing or physical-pixel sizes may pass for the wrong reason. `reward_model_test.gd`
   already notes headless `push_input` hit-testing as unreliable.
